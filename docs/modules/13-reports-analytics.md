# Module: Reports & Analytics

## Overview (Workflow 11)

Dashboards and downloadable reports for administrators and accounts staff.

## Report categories (from doc)

### Examination
- Summary, published exams, schedule, status, statistics

### Student
- Eligible, registered, hall ticket issued, absent, history

### Fee
- Collection summary, pending, paid, late fee, offline/online

### Financial
- Daily/monthly collection, gateway summary, cash/bank, revenue

### Results
- Pass %, subject-wise, branch-wise, regulation-wise

## Filters

Examination, Regulation, Course, Branch, Batch, Semester, Date Range

## Export formats

PDF, Excel
